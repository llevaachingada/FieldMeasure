## A. IT already installed it

**Subject:** New on your Surface: Field Measure (photo + dimensions)

Hi all,

There's a new app on your Surface called **Field Measure**. Find it in the Start menu.

It replaces paper sketches: take a photo of the site, draw the dimensions on it with your finger or
pen, and export a PDF to drop into the job folder in Dropbox. It works with **no signal**.

**The first time you open it (1 minute):**
1. Pick the hand you write with.
2. Tap **Use Documents\FieldMeasure**. If it asks for permission, choose **Allow on every visit**.

**Using it:** tap **New project** → name it → take the photo → **Use photo** → tap the
**Dimension** tool and tap the two ends → type the measurement. Tap **Help** at the top of any
screen for the short guide.

If you see **"Update ready"**, tap **Reload** when you're between photos. Your work is saved
automatically.

Problems or ideas? Tell <name>. A screenshot helps.

Thanks,
